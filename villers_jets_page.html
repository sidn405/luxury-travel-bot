Frequently Asked Questions

An empty leg flight occurs when a private jet needs to return to its home base or reposition for the next charter without passengers. These flights are offered at significantly reduced rates, often 50-75% less than standard charter prices.
Empty leg flights typically offer savings of 50-75% compared to regular charter prices. The exact discount depends on the route, aircraft type, and timing. These exceptional rates make luxury private jet travel accessible at a fraction of the usual cost.
Yes! We offer both full aircraft charters and individual seat bookings on empty leg flights. Shared seat options allow you to experience premium private jet travel while only paying for the seats you need, making it even more cost-effective.
Empty leg flights have fixed departure times and destinations since they are repositioning flights. However, we have access to thousands of empty legs across the globe, so there is often flexibility in finding a flight that matches your schedule.
Our team monitors empty leg availability in real-time across our global network of operators. Simply enter your desired route and dates, and we will notify you of matching opportunities. You can also browse our current listings above.
